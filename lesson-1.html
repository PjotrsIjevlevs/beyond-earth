A journey into the unknown

The Crystal Egg

What if an ordinary object could show you another world?

LEVEL B1–B2  ·  LISTENING  ·  SCIENCE FICTION
01 · A little introduction

Before you listen

Imagine finding a strange object in an old shop. It looks like a crystal, but something seems to move inside it. Would you keep it, sell it, or try to discover its secret?

In this story, Mr Cave owns a curious crystal egg. When he looks into it, he sees something extraordinary: another world.

This is a modern audio adaptation by Paul Alvis, based on the short story by H. G. Wells. The adaptation changes the original setting and several elements of the plot.

02 · Your vocabulary notebook

Useful expressions

Take a moment to read these expressions before listening. You will use them in the activities later.

to make a fortune To earn or receive a lot of money.
to be behind on the bills To have unpaid bills because you do not have enough money.
to dismiss something To decide that something is not important or not true.
in real time At the same moment that something is happening.
priceless So valuable that it is difficult or impossible to give it a price.
to vanish To disappear suddenly.
03 · Press play

The Listening Room

Find a comfortable place, put on your headphones and listen to the story. The audio is approximately 28 minutes long.

A listening tip

You do not have to understand every word. Focus on the main events, the characters and the changes in the story. If 28 minutes feels too long, listen in two or three parts.

04 · A moment to reflect

After listening

Before moving on, think about these questions. You will explore them in more detail in Mission Control.

  1. What exactly can Mr Cave see inside the crystal egg?
  2. Who helps him investigate the strange object?
  3. What happens to the signals from the other world?
  4. Does the crystal egg make Mr Cave's life better?

You do not need to write anything here. Just think about your answers.
