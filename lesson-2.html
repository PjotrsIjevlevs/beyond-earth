01 · Follow the clues

Investigate the story

Answer in complete sentences. Try to use your own words rather than copying the audio transcript.

1. The mysterious object

What is unusual about Mr Cave's crystal egg? What can he see inside it?

2. A helping hand

Who is Dr Terry Santos, and how does he become involved in the mystery?

3. Contact with another world

What happens when the scientists begin receiving signals? Describe at least two things they discover.

4. The ending

What happens to the crystal egg? How does Mr Cave's situation change by the end of the story?

02 · Decode the language

Words from another world

Complete each sentence with the correct expression from the word bank. You may need to change the form of a word.

6. The signals suddenly __________.

03 · Think beyond the story

Explore the bigger picture

There are no single correct answers here. Explain your ideas and support them with details from the story.

1. The power of social media

A schoolboy records the crystal egg and posts the video online. At first, many people think it is fake. How does social media influence the events in the story?

2. A difficult decision

Mrs Cave wants to sell the crystal egg. Do you understand her decision? What would you do in her situation?

3. What happens next?

Imagine that the signals from the other world return tomorrow. What do you think will happen next? Write a short prediction.

04 · The Common Room

One question, many perspectives

Let's bring the discussion beyond the story.

What would be the biggest challenge if humanity discovered intelligent life on another planet?

Consider communication, science, politics, religion, technology or everyday life. Explain your opinion and give examples.
